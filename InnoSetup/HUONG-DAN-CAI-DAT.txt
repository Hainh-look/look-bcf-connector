========================================================================
            HUONG DAN CAI DAT LOOK BCF - REVIT CONNECTOR
========================================================================

Phien ban: 1.0.0
Tuong thich: Autodesk Revit 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026
May chu ket noi: https://bim.lookbim.com (OpenProject BIM - Chuan BCF 2.1)

------------------------------------------------------------------------
CACH 1: CAI DAT BANG GOI SETUP TU DONG (.EXE)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Chay file "LookBcf-Setup-v1.0.0.exe".
2. Trinh cai dat se hien thi giao dien huong dan.
3. Chon cac phien ban Revit can cai dat (mac dinh se chon cac ban da cai tren may).
4. Nhan "Next" va "Install".
5. Hoan tat va mo Autodesk Revit.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
CACH 2: CAI DAT BANG GOI PORTABLE 1-CLICK (.ZIP)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
(Khong can quyen Administrator, khong lo bi chan boi chinh sach IT)

1. Giai nen file "LookBcf-Revit-v1.0.0-Portable.zip" vao mot thu muc bat ky tren may.
2. Nhan dup chuot vao file "Install-LookBcf.bat".
3. Script se tu dong copy cac thu vien tuong thich:
   - Revit 2021 - 2024: chay tren .NET Framework 4.8
   - Revit 2025 - 2026: chay tren .NET 8.0 Windows
4. Thong bao "DA CAI DAT THANH CONG" xuat hien -> Nhan phim bat ky de dong.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
HUONG DAN SU DUNG TREN REVIT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Mo phan mem Autodesk Revit (bat ky ban nao tu 2021 den 2026).
2. Neu Revit hien hop thoai hoi "Security - Unsigned Add-In", chon "Always Load".
3. Tren thanh Ribbon menu tren cung, click vao tab "Look BCF".
4. Click nut "Look BCF" de mo bang dieu khien ket noi:
   - Bang se mo ben hông Revit (Dockable Panel) hoac cua so noi tien dung.
   - Dang nhap tai khoan may chu Look BIM (https://bim.lookbim.com).
   - Xem danh sach van de (Issues / Topics), nhan tao Viewpoint tu mo hinh hoac nhay truc tiep vao goc nhin camera trong Revit!

------------------------------------------------------------------------
GO CAI DAT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Voi goi .EXE: Vao Windows Settings -> Apps -> "Look BCF for Autodesk Revit" -> Uninstall.
- Voi goi Portable: Nhan dup vao file "Uninstall-LookBcf.bat".
========================================================================
